### Product description

Nine books of classical Chinese imperial chronicle set in Richmond, British Columbia. Hospital gowns become "garments of golden thread." Psychiatric restraints become "leather straps to pacify the mind." The RCMP becomes the Royal Constabulary Mounted. The Grand Historian and Confucius annotate with the same gravity they'd apply to a succession crisis.

This is not satire. This is a chronicle — claiming that events in a Vancouver suburb deserve the same form as the Han dynasty annals.

Trilingual edition: classical Chinese source text with English and French translations. 9 analects, 3 doctrine essays, illustrations. Free to read at [annihilism.org](https://annihilism.org). Pay here for the PDF and EPUB.

**Egregoregramming 101** is a six-week course in designing autonomous AI avatars that own wallets, hold memory, sign transactions, refuse requests in-character, and keep their face from drifting back to the median.

If you've ever wanted to build an AI that feels like a person instead of a customer service script, this is the manual.
